## Locked hierarchy

- **AI Creates AI** is the parent company building persistent AI organizations for businesses.
- **EVE CXO** is the commercial customer product.
- **Eve** is the customer-facing executive intelligence inside EVE CXO.
- **Adam** is AI Creates AI's internal executive intelligence, not a customer product.
- **Hermes** is shared durable orchestration/runtime infrastructure, not a separately marketed product.
- **Jarvis** is a shared development capability for internal and appropriate customer build workflows, not a ninth EVE CXO department.
- **OpenClaw** may be used as a conversational layer where appropriate; it is not positioned here as a competing public product.
